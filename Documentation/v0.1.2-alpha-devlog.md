### Fence
Right now the fence is made of 3 cube actors stretched out, so I'm going to have to upgrade that. First, I need to see what kind of architecture we are looking at for early 1900s Australia.

![[Pasted image 20251209123144.png]]
> [Original Yulgilbar Homestead, property of E.D.S. Ogilvie, ca.1852, by Ellen Bundock. Oil on panel. PXA 2693](http://acms.sl.nsw.gov.au/item/itemDetailPaged.aspx?itemID=442045 "Catalogue link")

Looks like just simple wooden fencing. I can make two styles, one from sticks/logs for less total occlusion, and one from planks for more occlusion.

From the 1850s, corrugated iron and stone became more commonplace in domestic housing. I could also go for a corrugated iron fence, sparingly. [Source](https://sahistoryhub.history.sa.gov.au/subjects/building-materials/)

Let's just do one style for now.
##### Fence 1
I threw a couple of cylinder meshes into Blender.
![[Pasted image 20251209125128.png]]

Then, I applied the array modifier, joined the meshes, and unwrapped the UVs. The poles aligned nicely with the V-axis so my wood texture will have grains along the pole axis too.
![[Pasted image 20251209125322.png]]

For my wood texture I created a 768x768 canvas to visualise 3x3 tiling, and set up Symmetry Tiling in GIMP for 256x256 tiles.
![[Pasted image 20251209125826.png]]

I ended up with something like this.
![[Pasted image 20251209131516.png]]

I then exported the mesh and 256px texture out and then into Unreal.
![[Pasted image 20251209132432.png]]

What you see here is the 265px texture stretched over the entire UV square, I want to scale it down so we can see the grains and eye and all that detail. I added a `ScaleUVsByCenter` node with a scalar parameter. I also didn't like how intense the colour was so I just multiplied it by 0.4. We end up with something like this.
![[Pasted image 20251209133017.png]]![[Pasted image 20251209133031.png]]
 
Looks ok for a basic prop :)

### Windmill
The windmill model plays with symmetry in Blender. It consists of the support structure, and then a set of blades and a rudder tail. The latter two are combined as a single actor in the game, and the blades rotate at a constant rate. The animation is driven in Unreal by just rotating the actor.
